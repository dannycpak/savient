## Approved short disclaimer (in-app, every result screen)

> Visual Check is a second opinion based on a photo — not a certified authentication, appraisal, or guarantee of identity, treatment status, or value.

## Approved long form (Terms / help)

Sage Visual Check uses machine vision to offer a **non-binding second opinion** about a mineral specimen from a user-supplied photograph. Results may be incomplete or incorrect. Sage does **not**:

- certify authenticity, locality, treatment status, or provenance;
- provide a formal appraisal or insurance valuation;
- replace inspection by a qualified mineralogist, gemologist, or laboratory.

Purchase and collecting decisions remain solely the user’s responsibility. Price ranges, when shown, are illustrative placeholders until comparable-sales data is available and must not be treated as market quotes.

## UI requirements (non-negotiable)

1. The short disclaimer **must** appear on every Visual Check result screen before or with “Save to catalog”.
2. Do not use language such as “authenticated”, “verified authentic”, “certified”, or “guaranteed ID” in Visual Check UI or push copy.
3. Paywall and marketing may describe Visual Check as a “second opinion” only.

## Change control

Any edit to `VISUAL_CHECK_DISCLAIMER` requires a new legal review pass and an update to this document’s “Last reviewed” date.
