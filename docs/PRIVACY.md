# Privacy Policy — Sage

**Last updated:** 2026-07-15  
**Contact:** privacy@sage.app *(replace before public launch)*  
**Operator:** Sage *(replace with legal entity name)*

This Privacy Policy describes how Sage (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Sage mobile application.

## 1. Information we collect

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Email, display name | Create and manage your account |
| Collection data | Specimen records, notes, estimated values | Provide catalog features |
| Photos | Specimen and Visual Check images | Catalog + Visual Check (AI second opinion) |
| Usage | Feature interactions, device type (coarse) | Operate and improve the app |
| Purchases | Subscription / credit entitlements via Apple/Google & RevenueCat | Provide Sage+ and check credits |

We **do not** intentionally collect precise GPS location. Photos are re-encoded on-device before upload to strip EXIF/GPS metadata.

## 2. How we use information
- Provide catalog, Visual Check, account, and (future) marketplace features
- Enforce free-tier limits and Sage+ entitlements
- Secure the service, prevent abuse, and comply with law
- Communicate about account or security issues

Visual Check sends images you submit to our servers and to our AI provider (Anthropic) solely to generate a second-opinion result. Results are stored in your account so you can review them.

## 3. Sharing
We share data with processors that help us operate Sage (e.g. Supabase for database/auth/storage, Anthropic for vision inference, RevenueCat / Apple / Google for in-app purchases). We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Retention
- Account and catalog data: retained while your account is active
- Deleted accounts: soft-deleted immediately; permanently purged within **30 days** (storage objects and auth user removed)
- Purchase records may be retained as required for accounting / store compliance

## 5. Your rights
Depending on your region, you may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal information. In-app: Account → Delete account. Or email privacy@sage.app.

## 6. Children
Sage is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required in your jurisdiction).

## 7. Changes
We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted by updating the “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, in-app notice.
